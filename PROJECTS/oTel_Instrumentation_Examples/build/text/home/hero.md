# OpenTelemetry, in four languages.

A cookbook of small, copy-and-go recipes for emitting OpenTelemetry traces, metrics, and logs from JavaScript, Python, .NET, and Java. Recipes use the OTLP transport and work against any OTLP destination — including Dynatrace, a local OpenTelemetry Collector, or any other OTLP-compatible backend.

[Configure your OTLP destination](#){.cfg-link data-cfg-open} once and every recipe on this site renders with your endpoint, headers, and service name pre-filled.
