# Scenarios — intros, checklists, summaries

## Remove the alligator
- Intro: "Watch what happens when the apex predator is removed. The food web rebalances over a few simulated weeks."
- Checklist:
  1. "Snake population rises by 20% above baseline."
  2. "Bass population rises by 35% above baseline."
  3. "Heron population rises by 18% above baseline."
  4. "Tadpole population falls by 25% below baseline."
- Summary template: "Removing the alligator allowed snakes ({snakeΔ}), bass ({bassΔ}), and herons ({heronΔ}) to expand. Their combined pressure pushed tadpoles down by {tadpoleΔ}."

## Mosquito explosion
- Intro: "Begin with a tenfold mosquito surge. Watch their predators respond — and what is lost when they cannot."
- Checklist:
  1. "Mosquitoes rise to at least 5× baseline within the first month."
  2. "Dragonfly population rises by 30%."
  3. "Bat or frog population rises by 20%."
  4. "Mosquitoes return to within 1.5× of baseline."
- Summary template: "Mosquitoes peaked at {peak}× baseline before predator response brought them back to {final}× over {days} days."

## Algae bloom
- Intro: "A nutrient surge feeds the algae. Watch the oxygen — and the fish that depend on it."
- Checklist:
  1. "Algae mass rises to at least 4× baseline."
  2. "Oxygen drops below 0.5 of normal."
  3. "Bass population falls by at least 30%."
  4. "Snail population rises by at least 20% (grazing the algae)."
- Summary template: "Algae bloomed to {peak}×, oxygen fell to {minO}, bass dropped {bassΔ}; snails grazed back the bloom over {days} days."

## Beaver dam
- Intro: "A beaver pair begins building. Watch the water rise and the wetlands expand."
- Checklist:
  1. "Dam reaches 100% completion."
  2. "Water level rises by at least 30%."
  3. "Aquatic species (bass, frog, dragonfly nymph) rise by at least 15% combined."
  4. "Shoreline plant patches expand by at least 25%."
- Summary template: "The dam raised water by {waterΔ}; aquatic populations grew by {aquaticΔ} as wetlands expanded."

## Drought year
- Intro: "Rains fail. Watch the water shrink — and where life retreats. Notice what the alligator does."
- Checklist:
  1. "Water level falls to 0.5× baseline."
  2. "At least one gator-hole is established."
  3. "Fish population falls by at least 40%."
  4. "At least one species takes refuge near a gator-hole."
- Summary template: "Drought reduced water to {minW}; alligators maintained {nHoles} gator-holes that sustained {refugeCount} species through the dry months."
