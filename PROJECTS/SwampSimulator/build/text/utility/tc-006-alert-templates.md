# Alert templates

Substitute {species}, {pct}, {event}, {scenario} at runtime.

- Extinction: "{species} went extinct on day {day}."
- Near extinction: "{species} dropped below 10% of baseline."
- Recovery: "{species} returned to within 90% of baseline."
- Event start: "{event} began."
- Event end: "{event} ended."
- Scenario complete: "{scenario} — all checklist items observed."
- Save quota warning: "Save data is approaching the 5 MB limit."
