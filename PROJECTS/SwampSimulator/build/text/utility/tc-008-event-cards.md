# Environmental event cards

Each card shows a title and a one-line description.

- Drought
  - "Lowers the water level and stresses aquatic species."
- Flood
  - "Raises the water level and displaces shoreline animals."
- Pollution
  - "Taints the water; sensitive species lose energy faster."
- Fire
  - "Burns shoreline plants until the event clears."
- Nutrient runoff
  - "Spikes nutrients and triggers fast algae growth."
- Cold snap
  - "Drops temperature; cold-blooded species slow down."
