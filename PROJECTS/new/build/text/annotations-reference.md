## Hook-First

> The curiosity gap pulls the reader past the first line. By withholding the key insight, the opening creates a tension that only resolution satisfies. Engagement follows the gap, not the answer.

(Word count: 36)

---

## Controversy-Max

> Provocative framing triggers the instinct to respond. Counterintuitive claims break the scroll habit — readers pause to agree, disagree, or share. The stance is clear enough to provoke, restrained enough to avoid polarisation.

(Word count: 37)

---

## Authority

> Cited expertise anchors the message in credibility. Precise figures replace vague claims. Readers who trust the source are more likely to share the content as a credible reference rather than an opinion.

(Word count: 35)

---

## Story-Mode

> Narrative arc sustains attention beyond the first sentence. Setup creates context, tension creates investment, resolution delivers the payoff. First-person immediacy bridges the gap between writer and reader.

(Word count: 30)

---

## Minimalist

> Radical brevity creates signal. Every word that remains carries more weight. The white space around a concise post on X increases perceived confidence and invites engagement without demanding it.

(Word count: 32)
