## Strategy Full Descriptions (used in onboarding or help text)

### Hook-First
Leads with the most surprising element. The opening creates a curiosity gap — the reader must continue to close it. Best for ideas that have a hidden twist or counter-intuitive insight.

### Controversy-Max
Takes a clear, counterintuitive stance. Challenges conventional wisdom to trigger a response. Uses provocative framing without relying on anger or division.

### Authority
Grounds the message in credentials, data, or consensus. Uses precise figures and references the nature of evidence. Best when the original content has factual weight to leverage.

### Story-Mode
Structures the message as a three-beat arc: setup, tension, resolution. Uses first or second person to create immediacy. Best for experiential or emotional content.

### Minimalist
Reduces the idea to its irreducible core. Targets 140 characters or fewer where the idea allows. Uses silence and whitespace as rhetorical tools.
