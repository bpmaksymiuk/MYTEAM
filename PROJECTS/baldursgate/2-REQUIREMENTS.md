# Business Requirements

**Project:** Baldur's Gate (Recreation)
**Source:** 1-USE-CASES.md (UC-001 – UC-015)

---

## UC-001: Launch Game and Main Menu

**BR-001** — The game shall display a main menu on launch containing four options: New Game, Load Game, Options, and Quit.

**BR-002** — Selecting New Game shall navigate to the character creation screen.

**BR-003** — Selecting Load Game shall present the list of saved game slots; selecting a slot loads that save.

**BR-004** — Selecting Options shall open the options configuration screen.

**BR-005** — Selecting Quit shall exit the application immediately.

---

## UC-002: Create a Character

**BR-006** — The character creation screen shall allow the player to enter a character name (text input, maximum 30 characters).

**BR-007** — The player shall be able to select gender: Male or Female.

**BR-008** — The player shall be able to select a race from: Human, Elf, Dwarf, Halfling, Gnome, Half-Elf, Half-Orc.

**BR-009** — The player shall be able to select a class from: Fighter, Mage, Thief, Cleric, Druid, Ranger, Bard, Paladin, Sorcerer, Shaman.

**BR-010** — Race and class combination restrictions shall be enforced per AD&D 2nd Edition rules (e.g., Dwarves cannot be Mages or Paladins).

**BR-011** — Ability scores (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma) shall be generated by a dice-roll mechanism; the player may re-roll freely before confirming.

**BR-012** — The player shall be able to select a character portrait from a set of preset images.

**BR-013** — Confirming character creation shall begin the game in the starting area.

---

## UC-003: Explore the World (Isometric View)

**BR-014** — The game world shall be rendered as a 2D isometric pre-rendered backdrop with sprites for characters and objects.

**BR-015** — Clicking a walkable tile shall command the whole party to pathfind and move to that destination using A* or equivalent pathfinding.

**BR-016** — The camera shall scroll when the mouse cursor is within 20 pixels of the screen edge, and via middle-mouse-button drag.

**BR-017** — Areas shall have collision maps defining impassable tiles (walls, water, obstacles).

**BR-018** — The game shall render characters and objects on top of the background using depth-sorted sprite order (lower Y = drawn later).

---

## UC-004: Engage in Combat

**BR-019** — When a hostile creature is in range and the party is not paused, combat shall proceed in real-time.

**BR-020** — The player shall be able to pause and unpause combat at any time using the Spacebar.

**BR-021** — Attack rolls shall be calculated as: 1d20 vs. attacker's THAC0 minus target's Armour Class (AC), per AD&D 2e rules. A roll that meets or exceeds the required number is a hit.

**BR-022** — Damage on a hit shall be rolled per weapon's damage die and applied to the target's current HP.

**BR-023** — Saving throws (vs. Death, Wands, Polymorph, Breath, Spells) shall be resolved by rolling 1d20 against the character's saving throw target per class and level table.

**BR-024** — The player shall be able to command a character to: Attack (designate target), Cast Spell, Use Item, Use Ability, Move to Location, or Stand Ground.

**BR-025** — A character whose HP reaches 0 falls unconscious and cannot act; at –10 HP the character dies.

**BR-026** — Combat ends when all enemies in the area are defeated, flee, or the party leaves the area.

---

## UC-005: Recruit and Manage a Party

**BR-027** — The active party shall contain a maximum of 6 characters including the player character.

**BR-028** — Recruitable NPC companions shall be encountered in defined world locations and joinable via dialogue.

**BR-029** — A party member can be dismissed via the party panel; dismissed NPCs return to a designated location.

**BR-030** — Each party member shall have an AI behaviour mode selectable from: Aggressive, Defensive, Passive, and Custom (player-defined script).

**BR-031** — The party panel (displayed as character portraits) shall show each member's current HP and status icons.

---

## UC-006: Manage Inventory

**BR-032** — Each character shall have an inventory screen showing equipped slots: helmet, armour, cloak, gloves, boots, belt, amulet, two ring slots, main-hand weapon, off-hand weapon/shield, and four quick-item slots.

**BR-033** — Each character shall have a bag inventory of at least 20 item slots.

**BR-034** — Items shall be draggable between inventory slots, equipped slots, and between party members.

**BR-035** — Carrying weight shall be tracked per character based on Strength; exceeding the limit shall slow movement.

**BR-036** — Unidentified items shall display as "Unidentified [item type]" and cannot have their properties inspected until identified.

**BR-037** — Gold shall be tracked as a shared party resource displayed in each character's inventory screen.

---

## UC-007: Talk to NPCs and Make Dialogue Choices

**BR-038** — Clicking on a talkable NPC shall open a dialogue window showing the NPC's portrait, name, spoken text, and up to 6 numbered player response options.

**BR-039** — Selecting a dialogue response shall advance the conversation to the branching node defined for that choice.

**BR-040** — Dialogue outcomes shall be able to: set world state flags, start or update quests, grant or remove items, adjust reputation, trigger combat.

**BR-041** — Player reputation shall be a score from 1 to 20; it shall affect NPC reactions (hostile, neutral, friendly) and merchant prices.

---

## UC-008: Accept and Track Quests

**BR-042** — The journal shall display three tabs: Active Quests, Completed Quests, and Failed Quests.

**BR-043** — Each quest entry shall include a title, description, and a list of objectives with a completed/incomplete indicator.

**BR-044** — Quest objectives shall update automatically when the corresponding world state flag is set.

**BR-045** — Completing a quest shall grant defined experience points to all active party members.

---

## UC-009: Cast Spells and Use Abilities

**BR-046** — Mages and Sorcerers shall have a spellbook listing all known spells by level (1–9).

**BR-047** — Before resting, the player shall assign spells to memorisation slots per level per the character's class and level table.

**BR-048** — Casting a memorised spell depletes that memorisation slot; resting restores all slots.

**BR-049** — Spell targeting shall support: single creature, area of effect (circle or cone), line, and self.

**BR-050** — Area-of-effect spells shall display a targeting reticle before cast; the player confirms the placement.

**BR-051** — Thieves shall have skill points allocatable at level-up among: Pick Pocket, Open Lock, Find/Remove Traps, Move Silently, Hide in Shadows, Detect Illusion, Set Traps.

**BR-052** — Thief active abilities (e.g., Pick Pocket, Disarm Trap) shall be activatable via the ability bar and resolve a percentile skill check.

---

## UC-010: Visit Merchants and Shops

**BR-053** — Talking to a merchant NPC shall open a shop interface showing items for sale with their names, descriptions, and gold prices.

**BR-054** — The shop interface shall show the player character's current inventory and gold total.

**BR-055** — The player shall be able to buy an item if they have sufficient gold; gold is deducted on purchase.

**BR-056** — The player shall be able to sell items from inventory; gold is added equal to the item's sell value (typically 50% of buy price, modified by Charisma and reputation).

**BR-057** — Merchants shall offer item identification for a fixed gold fee (e.g., 100 gp).

---

## UC-011: Rest and Recover

**BR-058** — The player shall be able to initiate rest from a Rest button in the HUD.

**BR-059** — In areas designated as safe, resting shall simulate 8 hours; all party members recover HP (level × Constitution modifier formula) and restore memorised spell slots.

**BR-060** — In areas designated as dangerous, each rest check shall have a configurable probability of triggering a random encounter that interrupts rest.

**BR-061** — The player shall be able to assign a watch rotation before resting; characters on watch have a reduced chance of being surprised by a random encounter.

---

## UC-012: Navigate the Area Map and World Map

**BR-062** — The world map shall display all defined areas; areas not yet visited shall appear greyed-out or hidden until first entered.

**BR-063** — Clicking a discovered world map area shall initiate travel; travel between distant areas shall have a chance of triggering random wilderness encounters.

**BR-064** — Each area shall maintain a fog-of-war bitmask; tiles within the party's line-of-sight radius are revealed and remain revealed for that session.

**BR-065** — A minimap in the HUD shall show explored tiles of the current area and the party's current position in real time.

---

## UC-013: Level Up Characters

**BR-066** — Experience point thresholds per class and level shall follow AD&D 2nd Edition tables.

**BR-067** — When a character's accumulated XP meets or exceeds the next level threshold, the HUD shall display a level-up notification for that character.

**BR-068** — Confirming a level-up shall roll the character's class hit die (e.g., d10 for Fighter), add the Constitution modifier, and add the result to maximum HP.

**BR-069** — Spell-casting classes shall gain additional memorisation slots per the class level-up table.

**BR-070** — Thieves shall gain additional skill points to allocate at level-up.

**BR-071** — Fighters shall gain additional weapon proficiency slots at defined levels.

---

## UC-014: Save and Load the Game

**BR-072** — The player shall be able to save the game at any time (except in the middle of combat dialogue or cutscene) with a custom slot name.

**BR-073** — Up to 10 named save slots shall be available; a quick-save slot shall also be available (overwritten each quick-save).

**BR-074** — A save file shall capture: current area ID, party member data (stats, HP, inventory, memorised spells, XP), all world state flags, quest states, and discovered area bitmask.

**BR-075** — Loading a save shall restore the game to the exact state recorded, including area, party positions, door/container states, and world flags.

---

## UC-015: Configure Options

**BR-076** — The options screen shall provide volume sliders (0–100) for: Master Volume, Music Volume, and Sound Effects Volume.

**BR-077** — The player shall be able to set game difficulty: Easy, Normal, Hard, or Insane; difficulty modifies enemy HP multipliers and player damage dealt.

**BR-078** — Subtitles for NPC speech shall be togglable on/off.

**BR-079** — Camera scroll speed shall be adjustable via a slider (Slow / Medium / Fast).

**BR-080** — All options shall be persisted to a settings file and restored on next launch.
